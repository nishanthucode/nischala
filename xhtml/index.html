Foundations

Understanding True Yoga

When you utter the word Yoga, everyone has a notion of what it could be or should be. In other words, people have a lot of misunderstanding or misconception about the word "Yoga". One of the first things that people normally think when you say Yoga is that it means standing on your head, or twisting and turning the body into impossible postures. Some even believe that if you start doing Yoga, you will have to leave everything and everybody, become a Sanyasi, and settle down in an ashram or Himalayan cave.

"No, please understand, Yoga doesn't mean any of the above descriptions. Yoga is not about doing anything in particular. It is a way of being, it is a certain way in which a person can be. It is a state you can experience beyond body and mind."

Now there are many ways to reach this state. Isha Hatha Yoga is one of the ways. As there are tools and technologies to create and enhance external well-being, there are tools and technologies to create and enhance our internal well-being. Hatha Yoga is a science of asanas.
