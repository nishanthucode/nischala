About Nischala

Nischala was created with the intention of sharing timeless yogic wisdom in a simple and accessible way. In today's fast-paced world, yoga offers a powerful tool to create balance, inner stability, and wellbeing. We believe yoga is not just a practice on the mat, but a way of living consciously and harmoniously.

Mission

To guide individuals toward physical vitality, emotional balance, mental clarity, and inner wellbeing through authentic yogic practices.

Vision

To make traditional yoga accessible to everyone and inspire a healthier and more mindful way of living.

Yoga Philosophy

At Nischala, yoga is approached as a holistic science that nurtures the body, mind, and energy system. Our teachings are rooted in classical yogic principles while being practical for modern lifestyles. Isha Hatha Yoga programs offer an unparalleled opportunity to learn authentic classical Hatha Yoga practices that are derived from a yogic tradition and have been maintained in their purity and vibrancy for thousands of years. These practices are taught not merely for physical fitness and strength but as holistic processes toward mastery over the body and mind to manufacture health, joy, and bliss at will.

About Sadhguru and Isha Foundation

The classical practices offered are designed by Sadhguru, a yogi, mystic, and visionary, and a prominent spiritual leader. An author, poet, and internationally-renowned speaker, Sadhguru's wit and piercing logic widen our perception of life. He established the Isha Foundation, an international non-profit service organization, through which powerful yoga programs are offered as opportunities for self-discovery, inner transformation, and empowerment. Isha Foundation is run by volunteers inspired by their own personal transformation.
